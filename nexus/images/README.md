# Images

- `content.json` - the logos, the cross, and the title of the thumbnail and the page background, in the crossed template. The `nexus-mod-images` tool renders both images from it.
- `01-thumb.jpg` - 1920x1080, the first gallery image, so Nexus uses it as the thumbnail in the mods grid. The two intro logos on black, crossed out with one red painted X (the mark of a plague house), with the title "Skip Intro" in The Inquisition under them.
- `02-studio-logo.jpg` - 1920x1080, gallery. `src/splash-studio.png` scaled down: the Original Studios logo screen that the mod skips.
- `03-unity-logo.jpg` - 1920x1080, gallery. `src/splash-unity.png` scaled down: the "Made with Unity" logo screen that the mod skips.
- `banner.jpg` - 1300x372, the page background. The crossed logos of the thumbnail, smaller, in the center on black.

## Sources

- `src/splash-studio.png`, `src/splash-unity.png` - the two logo screens as the game drew them, captured from the game window at 3840x2160 on 2026-11-12, game version 0.12.7, with no mod loaded. Each is the frame with the logo at full brightness. The capture holds no mouse cursor and no Steam overlay.
- `src/x-paint.png` - the red painted X (1340x590, a PNG with alpha), drawn by a script of the dev workspace.
- The title font is The Inquisition by Woodcutter Manero (woodcutter.es). It is not in this repo: the render uses the copy installed on the machine. Its terms say free for personal use, with a commercial license from the designer.
